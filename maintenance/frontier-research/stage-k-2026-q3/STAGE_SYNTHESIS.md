Environment support floor and spec-expression generation jointly affect reproducible chart execution; a stricter TypeError is a different failure state from silently ignored arguments.
- matplotlib/matplotlib v3.11.2 ([release](https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib/releases/tag/v3.11.2)): PDF hexbin clipping and WebAgg/HiDPI rendering bug fixes. Static PDF export and interactive WebAgg/HiDPI output occupy different rendering surfaces, both version-sensitive.
