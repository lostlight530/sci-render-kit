# Stage B Month Reconstruction — 2024-05

## Evidence anchors

- Vega-Lite 5.18.1 released 2024-05-07
- CHI 2024 ran May 11-16
- Matplotlib 3.9.0 release notes dated 2024-05-15
- Umwelt, Customization is Key, and MAIDR are CHI 2024 research objects

## Story

May is the quarter's conceptual turning point

Renderer/version evidence continues through Vega-Lite and Matplotlib, but the accessibility papers move the object of study from one visual representation to a configurable multimodal system

Umwelt treats visual, sonified, and textual forms as coequal

MAIDR adds braille/review and user autonomy

Customization makes presence, verbosity, order, and duration explicit user-controlled variables

Thus communication state becomes:

    representation
    + modality
    + user configuration
    + interaction/query state

rather than one final image

## Boundary

Reported user studies are not universal accessibility certification and were not independently repeated here
