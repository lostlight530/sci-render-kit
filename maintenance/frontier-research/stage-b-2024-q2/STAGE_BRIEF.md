## Rationale

Stage A showed that a scientific figure is not exhausted by its bitmap and separated scientific/source evidence, visual encoding, render execution, and communication/accessibility evidence

Stage B asks what happened when Q2 systems made representation policy, multimodality, user control, renderer identity, and invalid-data handling increasingly explicit

## Research questions

1. How did Q2 plotting-library revisions make visual/interaction semantics visibly version-dependent
2. How did multimodal accessibility research move beyond a visual-first conversion model
3. How did user customization and invalid-data policy make communication state dependent on representation choices rather than one canonical view
