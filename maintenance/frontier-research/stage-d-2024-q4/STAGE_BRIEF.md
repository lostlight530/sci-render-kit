## Rationale
Stage C established that communication evidence is representation-, renderer-, interaction-, device-, task- and population-conditioned. Stage D asks how Q4 work makes assistive interaction paths, wrapper-provided screen-reader rendering, temporal animation state and accessible-color design separately visible.

## Research questions
1. What does Q4 accessibility research say about multimodal/LLM/touch/sonification interaction without implying universal accessibility?
2. When a wrapper adds a screen-reader-oriented renderer, what evidence does capability presence establish?
3. How do animation state and accessible color design expand communication provenance?
