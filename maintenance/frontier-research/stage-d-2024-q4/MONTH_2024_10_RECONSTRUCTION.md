# Stage D Month Reconstruction — 2024-10

October is an accessibility-interaction month.

ASSETS '24 and AccessViz work show multiple non-visual routes for chart exploration: LLM-mediated multimodal interpretation, touch/sonification, and screen-reader/sonification.

The key lesson is not that one system solves accessibility. It is:

```text
non-visual access
= multiple interaction contracts
with population/device/task-specific evidence
```

No universal semantic equivalence is inferred.
