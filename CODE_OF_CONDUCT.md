# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to Issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, and other public collaboration around this repository.

## Expected conduct

Communicate professionally and respectfully. Critique figures, claims, metadata, contracts, code, and methods rather than people. Protect private information, respect attribution and third-party rights, and keep uncertainty explicit. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation, fabricated evidence, deliberate deception, and repeated disruption are unacceptable.

### 图表证据诚信 / Figure-evidence integrity

**中文**：公开讨论图表必须准确说明数据来源、轴域、单位、不确定性、可访问性和实际验证边界，不能因样式美观或 PDF／PNG 成功输出就夸大科研有效性；未执行、不可用、失败与未测结果都是需要保留的研究状态

**English**: Public review of figures must preserve source data, axis domains, units, uncertainty, accessibility and actual validation limits. Attractive styling or successful PDF/PNG generation is not scientific validation. Unexecuted, unavailable, failed and untested outcomes remain legitimate research states

## Evidence-aware collaboration

Keep rendering success, metadata completeness, communication quality, scientific validity, external standards, proposals, and unknown state distinct. Do not present unexecuted checks as passed or silently rewrite historical maintenance records to match later knowledge.

## Sensitive information

Do not publish credentials, private data, or unpublished exploit details. Follow `SECURITY.md` for security-sensitive reports. Conduct concerns containing private information should use an available private contact route rather than a public Issue.

## Moderation

The repository maintainer may edit, hide, remove, lock, or reject contributions that violate these standards or materially undermine repository integrity. Repeated or serious violations may result in participation restrictions.

The repository owner retains final moderation and merge authority.
